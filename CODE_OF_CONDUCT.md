# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The NusaForge project aims to maintain a respectful, constructive, and welcoming environment for everyone who participates.

We value learning, collaboration, technical discussion, and different perspectives.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Focus discussions on technical ideas and project goals.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Accept reasonable criticism and differing opinions.
- Protect private information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or abusive language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or contributions.
- Other conduct that makes participation hostile or unsafe.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may review reported conduct and take appropriate action, including removing inappropriate content or restricting participation.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through an established contact channel.

Please include relevant context while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of personal information.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to interactions within NusaForge project spaces and when someone is representing the project publicly.
